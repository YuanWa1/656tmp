Description

This GLSL ES screen-space ray tracer renders a compact implicit 3D scene entirely in the fragment shader. It analytically intersects rays with planes, standalone spheres, and CSG sphere combinations, supporting union, intersection, and difference through interval/event-based inside-outside evaluation. The shader implements multiple material models including textured checker flooring, a procedural wall, reflective metal, transparent glass, and colored CSG solids. Reflection and refraction are traced iteratively with an explicit ray stack instead of recursion, using Fresnel-Schlick weighting and IOR tracking to split energy between reflected and transmitted paths through the glass medium. Direct illumination is computed from a mouse-controlled area light with multi-sample soft shadows, including partial shadow transmission through transparent objects. Miss rays sample a procedural sky/environment map with a sun highlight, and the final accumulated radiance is tone-mapped and gamma-corrected for display.
