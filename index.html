Description

This screen-space shader approximates Subsurface Scattering (SSS) for translucent materials by raymarching through a volume defined by screen-space depth and normal buffers. By iteratively stepping towards a dynamic, mouse-driven light source, it calculates light attenuation by accumulating the effective distance the ray travels inside the geometry to evaluate optical transmittance. The code then dynamically blends traditional directional surface illumination with a custom scattering intensity derived from the transmittance and surface normals. However, due to the inherent lack of back-face information in single-view depth maps, accurate volumetric thickness cannot be dynamically predicted; the internal boundary relies on a hardcoded depth threshold, which fundamentally compromises physical accuracy and limits the overall visual fidelity of the scattering effect.
