Soap Bubble Studio

Three hollow soap bubbles inside a box whose six faces all emit uniform white light. Each shell models air–soap film–air interference with a film IOR of 1.333. Static, warped Perlin noise varies the film thickness between 200 and 700 nm across each bubble, without texture seams.

Colors use a three-wavelength RGB approximation. Four samples per frame accumulate while the camera is still; moving resets accumulation to avoid ghosting. Keep the camera still to inspect a converged image.
